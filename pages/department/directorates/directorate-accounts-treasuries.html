Directorate of Account and Treasuries

Directorate

The Directorate of Accounts and Treasuries was established in the year 1972 and is under the administrative control of Finance (Establishment) Department, Government of Meghalaya. The Directorate is headed by the Director of Accounts and Treasuries who in turn is assisted by a Joint Director, Deputy Director and an Instructor of Central Training Institute, all from the Meghalaya Finance Service. The administrative hierarchy is further assisted in the performance of their duties by Accounts staff, technical staff for system management and a number of ministerial and Grade - IV staff.

District Treasuries

At the District level, there is one District Treasury in every District of the State with the exception of East Khasi Hills where there are two Treasuries. Each Treasury is headed by a Treasury Officer, a member from the Meghalaya Finance Service, assisted by (1) Assistant Treasury Officer/ Accountant, Senior Accounts Assistants and Junior Accounts Assistants, from the Meghalaya Subordinate Treasury Accounts Service. (2) Senior Assistant System Administrator (Technical) for system management and (3) Grade - IV staff.

Sub-Treasuries

Currently, there is one Sub-Treasury each in four Civil Sub-Divisions in the State. Each sub-treasury is headed by a Treasury Officer, a member from the Meghalaya Finance Service, assisted by (1) Accountant, Senior Accounts Assistants and Junior Accounts Assistants, from the Meghalaya Subordinate Treasury Accounts Service, (2) Assistant System Administrator (Technical) for system management and (3) Grade - IV staff.

Account units

Accounts unit were set up in every Cheque Drawing offices of PWD, PHE, Water Resources, Urban Affairs etc departments.. Each Account Unit is headed by the Accounts Officer and assisted by Senior Divisional Accountants and Junior Divisional Accountants, all from the Meghalaya Accounts Service. Accounts Officers cadre are under the Administrative control of Finance (Establishment) Department where as Senior Divisional Accountant (SDA) and Junior Divisional Accountant (JDA) are under the control of Directorate of Accounts & Treasuries. At present there are 9 Heads of Department (5 Cheque Drawing Authority and 4 Bill Drawing DDO), 2 Meghalaya Houses at Delhi and Mumbai, 18 Circles and 70 Divisions where Accounts Officers and Accounts staffs are posted. The sanctioned strength of these officers is as below:
  • Accounts Officer = 76 Nos.
  • Senior Divisional Accountant = 54 Nos.
  • Junior Division Accountant = 173 Nos.

Functions and Duties

The Directorate has both Administrative and Statutory functions as under
  1. Administrative control of all Treasuries and Sub-Treasuries
  2. Overseeing the functions of Treasuries as primary units of fiscal system of the State, regulating and facilitating the receipt of Govt. moneys and the withdrawal of govt. fund in accordance with the rules and regulations framed by the Finance Department and submission of monthly accounts to the office of the Accountant General (A&E) Meghalaya
  3. Overseeing the functions of all accounts units in cheque drawing Offices, viz., PWD, PHE, Water Resources, Urban Affairs, etc
  4. Administrative control of Meghalaya Subordinate Treasury Accounts Service;
  5. Administrative Control of Meghalaya Accounts Service;
  6. Training of Finance Officers, Accounts and Audit Officers, other Accounts & Audit staff, UDA & LDA of Heads of departments/ District offices/ District Councils. Etc., in all finance/accounts and audit matters.
  7. Nodal Officer in respect of New Pension Scheme.
  8. Cyber Treasury.
  9. Employee and Pension Database.

The duties of its officers and employees

Officers of the Directorate of Accounts & Treasuries and their duties
Officers Duty
Director The Director of Accounts & Treasuries (DAT) is responsible to supervise, control and affectively run the Directorate. All the decision making are taken at the level of the Head of Department except those that are not provided for, in different rules/instructions. The DAT is also the Administrative Head who controls the cadres of Meghalaya Accounts Service and the Meghalaya Subordinate Treasury Accounts Service. DAT is also vested with the responsibility of conducting training to the Officers of Meghalaya Finance Service, Accounts and Audit staff, Ministerial staff of the Heads of Department/District Officer etc. Various programmes are also organized for imparting training to the Officers and staff on the new initiatives and projects undertaken by the Directorate.
Joint Director of Accounts & Treasuries To assist the Director of Accounts & Treasuries on all matters aforementioned.
Deputy Director of Accounts & Treasuries To assist the Director of Accounts & Treasuries and Joint Director of Accounts & Treasuries on all matters stated above.
Instructor To conduct training to Officers of M.F.S., staff of the Accounts Service and Treasury staff in different capacities.
Treasury Officer The Treasury Officer is primarily responsible for financial transactions pertaining to receipts and payments on account of the State Government relating to various Government Departments. He is also responsible for timely and prompt submission of the monthly compiled Accounts relating to such transactions to the Accountant General. For proper discharge of the above functions, the Treasury Officer is to strictly observe all rules prescribed by the Government for his guidance with regard to the daily routine of Treasury work.
